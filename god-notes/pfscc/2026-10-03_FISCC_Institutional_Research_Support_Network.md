# PFSCC / FISCC — Institutional Research Support Network

**Timestamp:** 2026-10-03 19:55 HKT

## Why this matters

PFSCC / FISCC should become a priority domain because it has an unusually strong combination of:
- direct banking exposure;
- access to practitioners;
- institutional FX / fixed-income context;
- technical PBCT capability;
- cross-asset transferability.

A useful external benchmark is institutional FX / currency research at major global financial institutions. Practitioner feedback should be sought only through appropriate professional channels and should not be documented publicly as named personal relationships. The research itself should remain reproducible from public or appropriately licensed data.

## Domain map

Start with:
1. FX / currencies — spot, forwards, swaps, carry, basis, liquidity, hedging, flow, macro and microstructure.
2. Rates / fixed income / credit — curve, duration, spread, liquidity, funding, balance-sheet constraints.
3. Commodities — term structure, carry, inventory, seasonality, macro and physical-market links.
4. Crypto — 24/7 market structure, funding, basis, venue fragmentation and execution.
5. Equities — factor / stat-arb / flow / execution links.
6. Cross-asset — common risk factors, regime transmission, funding and liquidity.

## Supported research loop

**Public baseline -> institutional workflow map -> model / experiment -> practitioner critique -> revised artifact**

The goal is not to ask contacts for answers. The goal is to arrive with a serious hypothesis, data, model and limitations, then use appropriately obtained practitioner feedback to identify missing assumptions and real-world constraints.

## PACT × PBCT

PACT provides:
- access;
- context;
- institutional vocabulary;
- real problem selection;
- feedback channels.

PBCT provides:
- code;
- data;
- models;
- experiments;
- systems;
- reproducible artifacts.

The nonlinear edge is their combination.

## 2026-10-26 15:25 CST — Earn the Shirt: Football Roles × Financing and Capital Allocation
<!-- FOOTBALL-ROLE-CAPITAL-20261026-1525 -->

**Core analogy:** We want to get on the pitch and contribute. Different roles require different technical skills. Quant Research needs statistical validation and reproducible Python/SQL; Quant Developer needs engineering; Corporate Banking requires sales, client relationships, financial/credit judgment, product structuring, risk discipline and actual execution. S&P / Treasury coordinates data, financing economics and decisions. Networks create passing lanes; evidence-based judgment and delivery create goals. Football clarifies specialized positioning, while basketball also illustrates universal two-way offense/defense responsibilities.

**Capital decision:** A costlier player is not necessarily immediately substituted; a costlier strategy is not necessarily immediately cut. Separate +100bp SOFR *benchmark shock* from a +50bp prime-broker *contract-specific margin spread shock*. Hypothetical annual financing-cost increments: $20m repo at 100% SOFR pass-through **+$200k**; $15m PB margin at 75% SOFR pass-through plus 50bp PB spread **+$187.5k**; $10m stock borrow at assumed 20% SOFR sensitivity **+$20k**, total **+$407.5k**. Funding BPV for SOFR +1bp = **$3,325 annual expense**; PB margin spread +1bp = **$1,500 annual expense**. The stock borrow beta is a pedagogical hypothesis, not a known rule; spread widening is not assumed to affect repo or stock borrow.

**Five decision gates:** (1) check facility-specific terms and reset; (2) rerun forward expected *net* P&L; (3) assess market/liquidity/collateral/risk capital constraints; (4) test refinancing, netting, collateral changes, hedging and sizing; (5) retain, resize or reallocate based on *marginal risk-adjusted portfolio contribution* versus available alternatives. No automatic cut from the funding shock alone. If synthetic baseline net P&L were $500k/yr, post-shock estimate would be $92.5k/yr *all else equal*, without implying acceptable Sharpe, capacity or risk.

**Project-specific application:** PFSCC / institutional research support: connections/resources provide access to practitioner questions but cannot substitute for audited financial modeling or credit/risk constraints; route FICC/FX/financing artifacts and ask precise questions in relevant institutional conversations.

**English practice:** “I wouldn’t automatically reduce a strategy’s capital because funding has become more expensive. I would attribute the benchmark and spread shocks to the correct facilities, recalculate net expected returns and risk, then compare the strategy’s marginal contribution with funding alternatives and other opportunities.”

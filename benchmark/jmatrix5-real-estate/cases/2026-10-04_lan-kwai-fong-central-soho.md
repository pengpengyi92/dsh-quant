# JMatrix5 Case 002 — Lan Kwai Fong / Central–SoHo Fringe

**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Case type:** CBD + nightlife + residential-fringe real estate benchmark
**Reference case:** Case 001 — Wan Chai Station

## Node definition

Core:
- Lan Kwai Fong
- Wyndham Street / D'Aguilar Street
- Central CBD

Residential catchment:
- Graham Street
- Aberdeen Street
- Glenealy
- Shelley Street / SoHo
- Caine Road / Central–Mid-Levels edge

## Structural insight

Immediate Lan Kwai Fong is commercial / nightlife-heavy.
Residential supply becomes more meaningful around the surrounding Central–SoHo / Mid-Levels fringe.

## PACT

High hosting and client-entertainment density.
Suitable for:
- finance / professional-services worker housing;
- executive / relocation housing;
- office-to-home low-friction living;
- after-work relationship / hosting infrastructure.

## PBCT

Quantify:
- price vs walking time to Central;
- rent / sq ft;
- sale / sq ft;
- nightlife / restaurant density premium;
- slope / elevation;
- escalator / MTR access;
- rent yield;
- liquidity;
- employer-housing cost.

## PCCT

Study:
- international professional presence;
- local / expat nightlife interaction;
- work-to-social transition;
- quiet uphill residential pockets vs noisy commercial core;
- hosting / lifestyle fit.

## Comparison with Wan Chai

Wan Chai:
- broader residential stock around MTR;
- modern towers + serviced apartments;
- slightly more conventional residential / commuter structure.

Lan Kwai Fong / Central–SoHo:
- thinner residential supply in core;
- stronger nightlife / hospitality density;
- stronger direct Central-office walkability;
- residential stock shifts uphill / fringe.

## Decision

Use this case when evaluating:
- Central office jobs;
- finance / quant / bank roles;
- walking-to-office lifestyle;
- hosting-heavy professional roles;
- relocation to Central / SoHo / Mid-Levels.

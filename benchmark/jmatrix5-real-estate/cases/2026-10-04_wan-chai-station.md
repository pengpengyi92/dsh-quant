**Date:** 2026-10-04  
**Case origin:** live PMap field DD around Wan Chai MTR  
**Case type:** Station-centric residential / employee-housing benchmark

Wan Chai MTR Station and roughly 3–10 minute walking catchment.

Core use:

## 3. Rent ladder

- Room / flatshare: **~HK$13k**
- Small flexible studio: **~HK$16.5k**
- Modern studio: **~HK$20k–26k**
- Modern 1BR: **~HK$25k–33k**
- Modern 2BR: **~HK$28k–36k**
- Premium / larger: **~HK$48k–64k+**

## 4. Buy ladder

- Compact studio: **~HK$6M–7M**
- Modern 1BR: **~HK$7M–15M**
- Modern 2BR: **~HK$10M–13M**
- Premium 3BR: **~HK$13M–30M**
- Large premium family unit: **HK$40M+ possible**

Possible company structures:

A station-centric real-estate case is stronger than a single-building comparison because it connects housing choices to the full urban operating system.

## 11. JMatrix5 v1.1 — 30% Net-Salary Affordability Table

### Standardized assumptions

For comparability, this case uses the JMatrix5 Hong Kong 2026/27 benchmark:
- housing = **30% of disposable net salary**;
- disposable net salary = gross cash salary − Hong Kong salaries tax − employee mandatory MPF;
- single taxpayer, no dependants, no special deductions beyond mandatory MPF;
- basic allowance HK$145,000;
- progressive salaries-tax rates 2% / 6% / 10% / 14% / 17%, subject to the standard-rate comparison;
- employee mandatory MPF capped at HK$18,000/year for these salary levels.

### Reading the table

This table is deliberately conservative because the rent budget is tied to **post-tax, post-mandatory-MPF cash** rather than gross salary.

Examples:
- a HK$20k/month Wan Chai studio implies about **HK$67k/month disposable net income** and about **HK$78k/month gross cash salary** under the benchmark assumptions;
- a HK$30k/month unit implies about **HK$100k/month disposable net** and about **HK$118k/month gross**;
- a HK$50k/month premium unit implies about **HK$167k/month disposable net** and about **HK$198k/month gross**.

These are **single-income equivalent thresholds**, not statements about actual residents. Couples, housing allowances, employer-paid accommodation, bonuses, deductions and different tax circumstances can materially change the economics.

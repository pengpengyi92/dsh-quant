## Structural insight

Mid-Levels is a **vertical residential extension of Central**.

Its value is created by:
- proximity to CBD;
- escalator / walking connectivity;
- larger and quieter residential stock than the commercial core;
- close access to SoHo / Lan Kwai Fong;
- established professional / family housing.

## PACT
Housing / relocation / executive accommodation / hosting adjacency.

## PBCT
Rent and sale modeling, elevation, accessibility, time-to-office, yield, building age, school network.

## PCCT
Residential lifestyle, international professional presence, social / cultural transition between Central nightlife and home.

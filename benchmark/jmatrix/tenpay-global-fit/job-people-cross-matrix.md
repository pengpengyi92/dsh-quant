## JMatrix protocol

This file follows the dual-table protocol:

1. **Job Matrix** — what the organization hires for / needs.
2. **People Matrix** — who publicly leads, represents, or shapes those functions.
3. **Cross Matrix** — how people lineage and job architecture connect.

> Job Matrix tells us what the organization says it needs.  
> People Matrix tells us where authority / expertise publicly sits.  
> The cross tells us how the business likely operates.

# 7. JMatrix interpretation

## Structural insight

Unlike a hedge fund:

```text
Market Data → Alpha → Portfolio → Execution → PnL
```

TenPay Global looks closer to:

```text
Client / Merchant / Wallet Flow
          ↓
 Payment Network
          ↓
 FX / Pricing / Routing / Risk
          ↓
 Settlement / Partner Network
          ↓
 Conversion / Cost / Revenue / Experience
```

The **proprietary edge** comes from payment-network flow, client segmentation, partner quotes, routing and transaction context rather than purely public-market data.

- data modelling
